## How to Use the Subway Calorie Calculator

1. **Build your order.** Pick a menu category (including "Deals & Combos"), search for an item, and press Add. Use the plus/minus buttons to set quantity or the trash icon to remove an item. Anything missing — a regional special, a limited-time deal — can be added with the **Add custom item** row (name and calories; protein, carbs and fat are optional).
2. **Read your order nutrition.** The totals panel shows calories, protein, carbs, fat, sodium and fiber for the whole order, and the per-item breakdown lists what each line contributes, including its own walking equivalent.
3. **Enter your details.** Body weight, height (for stride length) and walking speed — nothing else. Switch between imperial and metric units with the toggle at the top.
4. **See your walking equivalent.** The result shows minutes (also as h:min), miles, kilometers and steps for the whole order. Everything updates live — there is no submit button. Use **Copy Results** to save the numbers, **Reset** to start over, or **Open Full Calculator** for incline, terrain and duration-based calculations.

## How the Walking Equivalent Is Calculated

The calculator reuses the same engine as our [walking calorie calculator](/) and [steps to calories](/steps-to-calories-calculator) tools, built on the 2024 Adult Compendium of Physical Activities. Each speed maps to a MET value (2.0 mph = 2.8, 3.5 mph = 4.8, 5.0 mph = 8.5, with linear interpolation in between), and net calories per minute are (MET − 1) × weight in kg ÷ 60 — subtracting 1 MET removes resting metabolism. A Gross/Net toggle shows both. Distance is speed × minutes, and steps come from stride length ≈ height × 0.414.

$$\text{Minutes} = \frac{\text{Meal kcal}}{(\text{MET} - 1) \times \text{weight}_{kg} / 60}$$

### Worked Example

A 6-inch Sweet Onion Chicken Teriyaki® is **430 kcal**. For a **160 lb (72.6 kg)** person at **3.5 mph**:

- Net kcal/min = (4.8 − 1) × 72.6 ÷ 60 = **4.6 kcal/min**
- Minutes = 430 ÷ 4.6 = **94 minutes** (1 h 34 min)
- Miles = 3.5 × 94 ÷ 60 = **5.5 miles** (8.8 km)
- Steps = 5.5 × ~2,220 steps/mile (at 5'9") = **~12,150 steps**

That is slightly more than the classic 10,000-step day.

## Where the Calories in a Subway Order Come From

Subway builds calories from four places, and knowing the split makes the menu easier to navigate:

- **Bread:** the base of every sub. A 6" Artisan Italian is 210 kcal and a 6" Hearty Multigrain is 200 kcal — bread alone is often 40% of a light sandwich's calories.
- **Protein:** surprisingly lean for most meats. Oven-Roasted Turkey adds just 50 kcal per 6" portion and Grilled Chicken 80 kcal, while Meatballs (250 kcal) and Tuna (250 kcal, from mayonnaise) carry far more energy density.
- **Cheese:** 80–110 kcal per serving (American 80, Provolone 90, Pepperjack 100, Monterey Cheddar 110).
- **Sauces:** the quiet calorie bomb. Mayonnaise is 100 kcal per serving and Baja Chipotle 70 kcal, while yellow mustard is 10 kcal and most vegetables are 0–5 kcal. Two mayo servings can add more calories than the turkey itself.

A 6" sandwich with lean protein, one cheese, mustard and loaded vegetables lands near 400–500 kcal; the same bread and protein with mayo, double cheese, a cookie (210 kcal) and hash browns (190 kcal) doubles the order without looking much bigger.

## Easy Ways to Compare Your Subway Options

Because the calculator updates live, use it as a comparison tool rather than a single lookup:

- **Swap the bread:** multigrain instead of Italian Herbs & Cheese saves 50 kcal per 6-inch (about 11 walking minutes at 3.5 mph).
- **Swap the sauce:** mustard instead of mayonnaise saves 90 kcal per serving (~20 walking minutes) with almost no flavor cost.
- **Compare formats:** the same Grilled Chicken build runs 300 kcal as a Fresh Fit 6-inch, 450 kcal as a salad and 620 kcal as a wrap — the larger tortilla is the difference.
- **Check sodium, not just calories:** several wraps and bowls exceed 2,000 mg sodium, the full daily target for many adults, while Fresh Fit 6-inch subs stay near 600–850 mg.
- **Watch portion multipliers:** footlongs, 12" wraps and double-meat options multiply every line item — the per-item breakdown shows exactly which item drives the total.

## Lower-Calorie Subway Order Ideas

- **Fresh Fit builds (270–310 kcal):** a 6" multigrain with Oven Roasted Turkey, mustard and all the vegetables — about 59–67 walking minutes for a 160 lb person.
- **Protein bowl (540–620 kcal):** skip the bread; a Turkey or Grilled Chicken bowl keeps protein high (37–48 g) and carbs near 10–13 g.
- **Broth-based soup + mini sub:** Chicken Noodle Soup (70 kcal) plus a Kids' Turkey Mini (170 kcal) makes a ~240 kcal meal — about 52 walking minutes.
- **Drop the cheese:** saves 80 kcal and 420 mg sodium per serving on a Turkey sub.

These ideas follow the same energy-balance logic as our guide on [how many calories are in a pound of fat](/blog/how-many-calories-in-a-pound-of-fat) — small, repeatable differences matter more than any single meal.

## Practical Takeaways

- **Use the walking equivalent as a unit of measure, not a debt.** 400 kcal ≈ 87 brisk minutes for a 160 lb adult is a useful sense of scale.
- **Footlongs are two meals:** a 1,000+ kcal footlong equals roughly 22,000+ steps for an average adult — split it or log it accurately.
- **Consistency beats precision:** a daily 30-minute walk at 3.5 mph is ~138 kcal net for a 160 lb walker, about 50,000 kcal a year.
